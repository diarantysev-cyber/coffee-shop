FROM FARM TO CUP

Meet our single origins

Distinct places, unique growing conditions, and a world of flavour in every cup.

01 / BRAZILCHOCOLATE · NUTS

Brazil Santos

Origin: Cerrado, Brazil

Altitude: 900–1,200 m

A comforting cup with notes of milk chocolate, roasted hazelnut, and caramel.

02 / COLOMBIACITRUS · HONEY

Colombia Supremo

Origin: Huila, Colombia

Altitude: 1,500–1,900 m

A balanced, bright coffee with orange zest, honey sweetness, and a clean finish.

03 / ETHIOPIAFLORAL · BERRY

Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

Origin: Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia

Altitude: 1,800–2,200 m

Delicate jasmine aroma, sweet berries, and a lively, tea-like finish.
